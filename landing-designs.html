Issue 01 · A tiny Mac essential

The app you'll forget you installed.

Most Mac apps want your attention. They live in your dock, your menubar, your notifications. They ask you to open them. Macaveli does the opposite. You install it once, give it accessibility permission, and it disappears. After the first week you stop noticing the icon at all.

What stays with you is the muscle memory. You stop reaching for the title bar — you hold ⌃⌘ and drag the window from wherever your cursor happens to be. You stop hunting for the corner — you hold ⌘⇧ and resize from anywhere on the surface. You snap halves with ⌃[ and ⌃]. You record screens with ⌃⌘R without breaking flow. By month two it stops feeling like an app. It feels like how your Mac works.

"macOS shipped without real window management. Macaveli is the part Apple forgot to ship."

# macaveli

Window management for people who live in their keyboard. Hold-modifier-and-drag from anywhere. Snap, maximize, record. No dock icon, no telemetry, no upsell.

## install

brew install --cask macaveli
# or grab the signed .app from releases ↓

## shortcuts

binding action
⌃⌘ + dragmove window under cursor
⌘⇧ + dragresize window under cursor
⌃[snap focused window left half
⌃]snap focused window right half
⌃⌘↑maximize
⌃⌘↓re-center
⌃⌘Rtoggle screen recording
