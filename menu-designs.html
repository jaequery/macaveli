Macaveli — Menu Variants

Five different takes on a hotkey-centric menubar UI. All ~380 pt wide. Pick the one that feels right and we'll build it.

Tip: every variant assumes you can press the shown key with the menu open to fire the action — clicks are a fallback, not the primary path.
Variant 1
Command Palette
Raycast-style. Open the menu, start typing, or press ↑↓ to navigate. Each row shows its hotkey on the right — but you can also fire by Enter, or by pressing the key combo while focused.

How they differ — quick decision guide

  • Command Palette — most familiar (Raycast/Spotlight). Best if you'll add more commands over time. Search-first.
  • Keyboard Map — most opinionated and Macaveli-native. Best at teaching the bindings spatially. Heaviest to build (requires a keyboard renderer).
  • Hotkey Cards — most marketable, screenshot-friendly. Treats hotkeys as the hero copy on each tile. Great default.
  • Cheatsheet — densest, lowest-chrome. Best for power users who already know what they want. Easiest to build.
  • Live Modifier — most novel. Genuinely "hotkey-centric" — the UI is driven by what you're physically holding. Risk: depends on real-time keystroke capture inside an open popover (doable, but the most engineering work).
